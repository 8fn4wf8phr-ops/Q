## Q | Resonance (musical intelligence)
_After v1.0. Depends on v0.4 memory._

- **Idea:** Q analyzes local song files, talks about them, has his own consistent musical taste, remembers what we listened to, and discovers music in a folder I approve. Fully offline.
- **Key constraint:** Q's language model can't hear. Songs are analyzed ahead of time into measurable facts (tempo, key, energy, density, sections, rough instrument guesses with a confidence level, similarity to other songs), and Q talks about those facts. Analysis runs in the background while Q is idle, never during a conversation. Local files only, no streaming services.
- **Honesty rules:** every opinion cites something measurable; instrument and mood guesses are hedged; Q never claims feelings or experiences.
- **Phases:** R1 drop in a song, Q gives facts and an opinion with reasons → R2 taste profile + listening memory → R3 "sounds familiar" similarity → R4 background discovery in an approved folder → R5 reacting to what's playing now.
- **Possible prototype later:** my Pulse music player already analyzes audio for its visualizer and could test R1 outside Q.
